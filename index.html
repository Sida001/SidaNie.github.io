Thesis and Notes

The Smooth Manifold Structure of Lie Groups. Undergraduate thesis.
An exposition of the differential-geometric properties of Lie groups, the correspondence between Lie groups and Lie algebras, and applications to SU(2). Supervised by Longting Wu.
Note: Gromov’s Almost Flat Manifold Theorem and Its Generalizations.
This is my note on metric geometry, based on lectures at a summer school in Hefei. Lecturer: Jikang Wang, USTC.
Explore Gromov's almost flat manifold theorem and related geometric results, focusing on equivariant GH-convergence, local groups,the Bieberbach theorem, Gromov theorem and nilpotent structures.
Notes on Riemannian Optimization. Reading notes based on Nicolas Boumal's An Introduction to Optimization on Smooth Manifolds. Covers first-order methods and convergence analysis. (Work in progress)
